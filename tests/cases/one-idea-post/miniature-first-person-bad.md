# Write the decision down

A decision nobody wrote down gets made again.

I used to think the answer was more process, and I was wrong about that. The fix was boring and it worked. The slowest 28 percent of requests took more than 46 milliseconds, which the average had hidden.

A weekly release of about 35 changes was easier to review than a monthly release of 59. A shorter path through the code made the next change cheaper as well. The billing team rebuilt the invoice service over 9 weeks and cut failed payments by 85 percent.

Refund tickets fell from 16 a month to 18 after the change shipped. Most of the delay came from a queue that nobody owned. The slowest 30 percent of requests took more than 44 milliseconds, which the average had hidden.

A weekly release of about 37 changes was easier to review than a monthly release of 57. Nobody had written down who could approve a refund, so every refund waited for the same two people.

I learned this the slow way, and my team paid for it.

Write it down today.
