# The next renderer

We built this because every team we worked with lost a week a quarter to slow previews. A weekly release of about 39 changes was easier to review than a monthly release of 55. The release notes got shorter because there was less to explain. The billing team rebuilt the invoice service over 13 weeks and cut failed payments by 81 percent.

## From pages to apps

Refund tickets fell from 20 a month to 14 after the change shipped. Customers noticed the faster checkout before the metrics did. The slowest 34 percent of requests took more than 40 milliseconds, which the average had hidden. The fix was boring and it worked.
